## Data we process

Depending on the user's role and actions, the service processes account and
contact information, farm and service addresses, precise location used for

## Why we process it

We use this data to authenticate users, provide drone-service booking and
fulfilment, assign pilots, show active-job location, process and reconcile
payments, send service notifications, prevent fraud, provide support, and meet
legal obligations. The app does not use personal data for cross-app tracking.
